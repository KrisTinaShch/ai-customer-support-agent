# Returns and refunds

## Return eligibility

Unused products can be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery.

The product must be returned with its original packaging, accessories, manuals, and proof of purchase.

Products with visible signs of use or missing accessories may not qualify for a full refund.

## Non-returnable products

Opened memory cards and other opened storage media cannot be returned unless they are defective.

Digital products, gift cards, and custom-made items are not returnable.

## Damaged or incorrect products

If a product arrives damaged or the wrong product was delivered, contact customer support within 48 hours after delivery.

Provide the order number, a description of the problem, and clear photographs of the product and packaging.

## Return process

Contact customer support and provide the order number and reason for the return.

Customer support will review the request and provide return instructions.

Do not send a product back before receiving the return instructions.

## Refunds

Returned products are inspected within 3 business days after they arrive.

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Original shipping charges are not refundable unless the product was damaged, defective, or incorrect.
