# Shipping and delivery

## Order processing

Orders placed before 14:00 on a business day are normally prepared on the same day.

Orders placed after 14:00, during weekends, or on public holidays are prepared on the next business day.

Processing may take longer during sales or periods of unusually high demand.

## Delivery methods and estimates

Standard delivery normally takes 2–4 business days after the order has been dispatched.

Express delivery normally takes 1–2 business days after dispatch.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and do not include the time required to prepare the order.

## Shipping costs

The available delivery methods and their prices are displayed during checkout.

Standard delivery is free for orders with a value of at least €100.

Express delivery is charged separately regardless of the order value.

## Order tracking

A tracking number is sent by email after the order has been dispatched.

Tracking information may take up to 12 hours to appear on the carrier’s website.

Customers can also check the current order status on the order tracking page.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed only before the order has been packed.

Contact customer support as soon as possible and provide the order number and the correct address.

The delivery address cannot be changed after the order has been transferred to the carrier.

## Delayed or lost shipments

If an order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, allow two additional business days before contacting customer support.

When contacting support, provide the order number and tracking number.

A shipment with no tracking updates for 10 business days may be considered potentially lost. Customer support will open an investigation with the carrier.
